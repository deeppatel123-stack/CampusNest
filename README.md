# CampusNest India — Student Housing & Rental Platform

A modern, full-featured student housing platform designed specifically for college and university students across India's top campuses (with benchmark coverage for **Nirma University, Ahmedabad**, DU North Campus, IIT Bombay, VIT Vellore, Symbiosis Pune, and more).

---

## 🌟 Core Student Features Built

### 1. 🎓 University & Campus-Centric Search
- High-speed typeahead autocomplete matching universities (e.g. **Nirma University, Ahmedabad**, IIT Bombay, DU North Campus, VIT Vellore, IISc Bangalore).
- Shows student hubs around each campus (e.g. for Nirma: Gota, SG Highway, Vaishno Devi Circle, Tragad, Chandlodiya).
- Displays average PG rent, total verified listings, and direct access.

### 2. 🏠 Real Verified Student Accommodations
- Detailed listings for **Boys PGs, Girls PGs, Co-ed Hostels, and Shared Student Flats (2BHK/3BHK)**.
- **Accurate Campus Commute Metrics**: Exact walking distance (e.g. `0.8 km • 6 min walk to Gate 2`), bicycle time, and auto fare savings.
- **Food & Mess Reviews**: Pure Veg & Jain meal schedules (Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Tea, Dinner) with meal frequency and quality ratings.
- **Transparent Pricing**: Monthly rent by room sharing type (Single, Double, Triple), security deposit, electricity meter rates, and zero brokerage.

### 3. ⚖️ Interactive Side-by-Side Cost Comparison
- Compare up to 4 PGs or flats side-by-side in a detailed matrix.
- Evaluates total first-month outflow (Rent + Deposit + Mess fees).
- **CampusNest AI Verdict**: Automatically flags the **Best Budget Value**, **Nearest to Campus**, and **Top Student Favorite**.

### 4. 🤖 AI Room & Budget Matchmaker
- 3-step interactive recommendation wizard:
  1. Select college & gender accommodation preference.
  2. Choose monthly budget tier & mess food requirements.
  3. Select #1 top priority (shortest walking distance, lowest rent, best food, or high comfort & AC).
- Produces ranked matches with compatibility scores (e.g. 98% Match) and detailed AI reasoning.

### 5. 📞 Direct Verified Owner & Warden Contacts
- Zero brokerage model: Students contact property managers and wardens directly.
- **1-Click WhatsApp Chat** with pre-filled property details.
- **1-Click Direct Phone Calling**.
- In-person free visit scheduling with confirmation.

### 6. ❤️ Shortlist Wishlist & Free Listing
- Save and bookmark accommodations in local storage.
- Dedicated free property listing portal for owners and wardens near campuses.
